FAQ

How do I play?

Watch the line of light in the middle of the screen. Tap when the gap of the next ring meets the line. Pass through the center of the gap for a “Perfect”.

Why did my light go out?

The glow around your light is its energy and it fades while you wait. Perfect passes refill it the most, Good passes a little — keep the rhythm going.

It's too hard. Can I make it easier?

Open Settings and turn on Assist mode (wider gaps, slower rings, slower energy drain) or the Safe moment cue, which highlights the line in gold at the right moment.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your device language. You can also pick one of 15 languages in Settings → Language.

There is no sound or vibration.

Check that your iPhone is not in silent mode, and that Sound effects, Music and Haptics are on in the game's Settings.

How do I reset my progress?

Main menu → About & Privacy → Reset progress.

Does the game collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
