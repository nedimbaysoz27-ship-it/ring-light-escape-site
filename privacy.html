English

RING: Light Escape ("the app") is developed by Nedim Baysöz. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services that collect data. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (stars, scores, unlocked orbs and themes) and settings (sound, music, haptics, language, accessibility options) are stored only on your device. We cannot access them. They are removed when you delete the app, and you can erase them at any time with About & Privacy → Reset progress.

Duel codes

When you finish a Perfect Chain Duel, the app creates a code on your device containing only the pattern number and your timing values in milliseconds. It contains no personal information and is shared only if you choose to share it yourself.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Send us a message.
